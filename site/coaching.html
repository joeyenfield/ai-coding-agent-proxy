Focused coaching

Ask the questions that matter to your work.

Review the whole session for the highest-value changes, or focus on one of these areas.

Prompts and workflow

Was the task clear? How much correction did it need? Get suggestions for a stronger prompt, when to plan first and when a fresh session could help.

Context and instruction files

Find instructions that could be shortened or loaded only when needed. Review context growth and ask for an outline of a leaner instruction file.

Tool use, failures and speed

Look for repeated searches, oversized tool results and failed commands. Ask for more efficient alternatives and what to check when calls fail or stall.

Cost and caching

Ask which habits or settings could reduce cost, where caching could help and whether a smaller model could handle part of the work. Savings depend on the task and provider.

Lessons for your project

Turn what the agent had to discover into suggested lines for your project’s instruction file, so future sessions start with useful guidance.

Your own review questions

Add a question when starting a review. Site admins can also edit the review prompts to match the checks and practices their team cares about.

Your choice of reviewer

Choose the model.
Understand what it receives.

Local models or hosted APIs

Choose an available provider that supports OpenAI Chat and uses the server’s credentials. Providers that require the agent’s own credentials are not offered. Paid-provider reviews incur model costs and count toward usage limits.

Selected evidence from the session

The model receives selected measurements, findings and short excerpts for the chosen topic. It does not receive the full transcript, file contents or whole tool results. Detailed checks require captured payloads.

The review suggests changes to your workflow; it does not automatically change your code or settings. Check its advice against your project. Cost figures are estimates, and a session review is not a correctness or security audit of the code.
