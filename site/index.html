A clearer way to run AI

Bring your AI operation
into one view.

As agents spread across teams and machines, visibility and coordination matter. AI Control Mesh gives you a shared place to manage the moving parts.

01 ◉

See agent activity

Follow sessions and requests, review model-call details, and understand how agents are using your AI services.

02 ⌘

Manage a machine fleet

Connect the computers running your models and agents. Manage providers, launch agents, and reach a shared terminal.

03 ⌁

Choose where AI runs

Route supported agents to local models on your machines or to hosted APIs. Mix providers to fit your setup.

04 ◎

Make it work for teams

Organize people into teams, share sessions intentionally, and grant access to the machines each group needs.
